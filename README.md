Hi, I'm Ayush - a curious coder and designer who enjoys building things people can actually use. My work sits at the intersection of software systems, real-time data, and physical infrastructure, with an emphasis on reliability, clarity, and thoughtful design.

I focus on creating well-structured applications and prototypes that translate raw inputs into meaningful, usable experiences. My interests span system observability, telemetry, and hardware-software integration, and I care deeply about maintainability, documentation, and clean abstractions that make complex systems understandable.

Outside core development, I explore hardware prototyping, PC systems, FPV drone technology, photography, and visual media. I'm drawn to technically dense projects that balance engineering rigor with aesthetic restraint - systems that work smoothly, explain themselves, and scale without unnecessary complexity.
